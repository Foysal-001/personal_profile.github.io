My Journey into Competitive Programming

Competitive Programming (CP) has been a vital part of my growth as a problem solver. Starting from simple algorithmic challenges, I gradually moved to solving complex data structures problems and participating in online contests.

Through platforms like Codeforces, I have solved hundreds of problems covering topics like dynamic programming, graph theory, and number theory. CP has taught me to think critically, optimize solutions, and work under time constraints — skills that are crucial not only in contests but also in real-world software development.

Participating in competitions also exposes me to different problem-solving approaches and coding techniques, enhancing my ability to tackle new challenges efficiently. For anyone pursuing a career in computer science, CP offers invaluable experience in logic, efficiency, and creativity.
